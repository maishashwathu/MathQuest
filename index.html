Challenge of the Week

Can You Crack This?

Difficulty: ★★★☆☆ Intermediate

A farmer has both chickens and rabbits. He counts 20 heads and 56 legs in total. How many chickens and how many rabbits does he have?

Hint: Set up two simultaneous equations using heads and legs.
Answer Revealed!

Let chickens = c, rabbits = r.
c + r = 20  &&  2c + 4r = 56
→ 2(20 − r) + 4r = 56 → 40 + 2r = 56 → r = 8
∴ 8 rabbits and 12 chickens. ✓
